Burning Elf

The Road to the 2027 Solstice

Hello everyone,

It is hard to believe, but spring and summer are fast approaching! While many people have begun planning their year ahead, several have asked me: is Burning Elf happening again this summer? As much as I would enjoy hosting another event this year, I have instead decided to take a pause. I intend on dedicating 2026 as a planning year with the ultimate goal of reimagining and relaunching Burning Elf during the 2027 summer solstice.

First, I want to share a heartfelt thank you to each of you. Looking back at Burning Elf 2025, the day turned out better than I ever could have imagined. Transforming a decade-old dream into reality was only possible because of your energy, your creativity, and the way you all showed up to celebrate LARP and to try something new.

The magic for me wasn't just in the game — it was the months of genuine creative flow leading up to it and seeing old and new friends connect through a shared activity. It fulfilled a long-term goal, but now that it has come and gone I've been asking myself how I can carry the torch forward. I'd like to share with you my concept for what I envision next, and would be so thrilled if you would endeavor to join me once again!

The Vision: Burning Elf 2027

For the next chapter, I am leaning further into my own Celtic ancestry and would like to re-orient Burning Elf around the summer solstice. Why the solstice?

Summer Solstice, sometimes known as Midsummer, Litha, or St. John's Day, occurs in the middle of June. It is a celebration of the longest day of the year and the beginning of Summer. It has been a grand tribal gathering time since ancient times. This is a sacred time for honoring Mother Earth and the Sacred Sun. Colors are Yellow, Green, and Blue. It is a festival of community sharing and planetary service.

-Circlesanctuary.org

  • The Theme: I want to celebrate my heritage from an ancestral perspective but also from a contemporary one. I wouldn't have found a LARP community if it hadn't been built by those who came before me. A summer festival centered on community sharing aligns deeply with how I've always envisioned Burning Elf. I'm imagining revelries such as feasting, music, dance, art, LARPing and a 🔥bonfire🔥.
  • A Community-driven event: Some of the most fun I had last year was simply seeing everyone gathered together, but I admittedly felt a bit stretched. I didn’t get to socialize with everyone nearly as much as I had hoped to! That said, my intention for the next Burning Elf is to make it a little more of a "potluck" style event. Of course, I intend to commit my own time and energy towards big-picture organizing, but would like to seek out a staff of organizers who will own various facets of the planning and event execution.
  • The Format: I'd like to move even closer toward a "Festival Ring" model. This actually ties back to the decade-old original concept of a weekend-long Burning Elf where it was imagined a central "Town Square" would be surrounded by a ring of "Embassies" or different local LARP groups hosting their own mini-games, recruiting players, and showcasing their worlds. I visualized the concept in the original Burning Elf logo, which depicts the burning elf hoisting up rays of light — the sunburst being the town square and the sun rays being the gathered LARP groups. In this vein I have designed a new Burning Elf logo which incorporates the same concept and is reimagined with a summer solstice theme.
  • A Change of Venue: As much as I will always hold a special place in my heart for Ravenna park, I would like to find a venue that is more suitable for a multi-day event. My current idea is to either reserve a state campground (like Fort Warden or Millersylvania), or possibly find a private venue (like Middleground). The basic format would be Friday for setup, Saturday for main-events and day-of visitors, and Sunday for breakdown.

Sustainability & The Hearth Fund

I'd love for Burning Elf to become a sustainable, repeatable tradition. To launch the 2025 prototype I invested my own funds — covering everything from permits and performances to the environment and "plot" items you interacted with. Let me take a moment to say Thank You to those who generously made donations afterwards to help pave the way for the next event!

To build a sustainable event, I am forming a Hearth Fund that will be kickstarted with my own contributions and, hopefully, in-part funded with community donations.

  • No Pressure Donations: I grew up in a LARP group that was free-to-play and I honestly want to carry that torch forward with Burning Elf. I am happy to commit what I can towards making this happen annually, but will humbly accept any financial support the community can provide. I intend on making a Hearth Fund report available to be transparent on event costs. If this feels like something you'd like to support, please reach out to me.
  • The Goal: Based on BE 2025, I expect event costs could be in the neighborhood of $6,000. Funds raised will cover the "hard costs" like private venue rentals and permits, logistics and food.
  • Non-financial Contributions: While the Hearth Fund will cover some costs, I encourage and will gladly accept support in other ways. Whether that be in the form of volunteering your time, sharing assets or logistics support, creating art or leading performances, I will happily take all the help I can get!
